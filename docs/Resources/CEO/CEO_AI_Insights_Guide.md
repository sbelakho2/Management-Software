# CEO AI Insights Access Guide

## Complete Access to All AI-Powered Insights

As CEO, you have **unrestricted access** to all 57 AI insight categories across 8 major areas. This access is locked at the system level and cannot be modified.

## CEO-Specific Features

### Persona Switching
View insights as any role to understand what your team sees:
1. Click your profile icon
2. Select "View as Role"
3. Choose any role
4. See their insight access

### War Room Mode
Access the Crisis Command Center for real-time multi-insight monitoring during critical situations.

### Board Report Generator
Automatically compile insights into board-ready presentations:
- Financial summaries
- Operational KPIs
- Risk assessments
- Strategic recommendations

## Rate Limits

Your access is virtually unlimited:
- **10,000 requests per minute**
- **100,000 requests per hour**
- **Burst: 500 in 5 seconds**

These limits support continuous dashboard updates and data-intensive analysis.
