# General Manager AI Insights Access Guide

## Comprehensive Operational AI Insights

As General Manager, you have broad access to operational, quality, and inventory insights to manage your facility effectively. You can monitor all aspects of day-to-day operations while having visibility into workforce productivity and financial summaries.

---

## Your Insight Access Summary

| Category | Access Level | Details |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| Production & Operations | ✅ Full | All production metrics and forecasts |
| Quality & Compliance | ✅ Full | All quality and compliance insights |
| Inventory & Supply Chain | ✅ Full | All inventory and supply chain data |
| Maintenance & Equipment | ✅ Full | Equipment health and maintenance |
| Financial & Cost | ⚠️ Summary | High-level cost metrics, not detailed margins |
| Workforce & HR | ⚠️ Partial | Productivity and attendance, not compensation |
| Sales & Customer | ⚠️ Summary | Pipeline overview, not detailed customer data |
| Strategic & Executive | ✅ KPI Dashboard | Facility-level strategic view |

---

## Your Insight Categories

### 🏭 Production & Operations (Full Access)

| Insight | What You See | Action Items |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Production Efficiency | OEE trends, throughput, cycle times | Daily production meetings |
| Bottleneck Analysis | Constraint identification | Resource reallocation |
| Downtime Analysis | Planned vs unplanned downtime | Maintenance prioritization |
| Capacity Utilization | Equipment and labor utilization | Shift planning |
| Production Forecasts | AI demand predictions | Capacity planning |
| Scheduling Optimization | AI-recommended schedules | Approve schedule changes |
| Real-time Status | Live production status | Immediate intervention |
| Yield Analysis | First pass yield, scrap rates | Quality initiatives |

**Best Practices:**
- Check Production Efficiency every morning
- Review Bottleneck Analysis weekly
- Monitor Downtime Analysis trends monthly

### ✅ Quality & Compliance (Full Access)

| Insight | What You See | Action Items |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Quality Metrics | PPM, defect rates, yield | Quality targets |
| SPC Analysis | Control chart insights | Process adjustments |
| Defect Predictions | AI quality forecasting | Preventive action |
| Compliance Status | Regulatory tracking | Audit preparation |
| Audit Readiness | Certification status | Gap remediation |
| CAPA Management | Corrective actions | Follow-up reviews |
| Customer Complaints | Issue tracking | Resolution oversight |

**Best Practices:**
- Review Quality Metrics daily
- Check SPC Analysis for out-of-control trends
- Monitor Audit Readiness before certifications

### 📦 Inventory & Supply Chain (Full Access)

| Insight | What You See | Action Items |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Inventory Levels | Stock levels, turnover | Inventory meetings |
| Reorder Recommendations | AI reorder points | Approve POs |
| Supplier Performance | Vendor scorecards | Supplier reviews |
| Supply Chain Risks | Risk alerts | Mitigation planning |
| Cost Optimization | Procurement savings | Budget decisions |
| Lead Time Analysis | Supplier lead times | Expedite decisions |
| Stock-out Predictions | AI shortage alerts | Preventive ordering |

**Best Practices:**
- Review Stock-out Predictions weekly
- Check Supplier Performance monthly
- Monitor Supply Chain Risks continuously

### 🔧 Maintenance & Equipment (Full Access)

| Insight | What You See | Action Items |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Equipment Health | Machine status | Maintenance scheduling |
| Predictive Maintenance | AI failure predictions | PM planning |
| Maintenance Costs | Cost tracking | Budget allocation |

## Rate Limits

Your access supports heavy operational use:
- **5,000 requests per minute**
- **50,000 requests per hour**
- **Burst: 250 in 10 seconds**

These limits support:
- Multiple dashboard tabs
- Auto-refresh every 5 seconds
- Shift change reports
- Ad-hoc analysis
