# Purchasing Starter Guide

## Sensei OS - Purchasing & Sourcing Complete Reference

---

## 1. Welcome & Role Overview

As a **Purchasing Specialist**, you are responsible for material availability, cost optimization, and supply chain risk management. In Sensei OS, you participate directly in the quoting process to ensure material estimates are realistic and risks are identified early.

### Key Responsibilities:
- Contribute to the **Quoting Helper** Stage-Gate workflow.
- Identify **Top Cost Drivers** and **Long Lead Materials**.
- Manage **Supplier RFQs** and select optimal offers.
- Monitor **BOM Obsolescence** (EOL/NRND).
- Maintain the **Approved Supplier List (ASL)**.

---

## 2. Industrial Quoting (Quoting Helper)

When a complex industrial RFQ is received, the Purchasing team must sign off on the **Purchasing Review Packet**.

### Tasks:
1. **Identify Cost Drivers**: Use the Purchasing work packet to flag the top 3-5 components driving the quote's price.
2. **Mitigate Long Leads**: If a part has a lead time >12 weeks, document the mitigation plan (alternate sourcing, bridge buy, etc.).
3. **BOM Intelligence**: Review AI-detected risks such as obsolescence or single-source dependencies.
4. **Sign Off**: Once the material baseline is validated, click **Sign Off** to allow the Estimator to finalize the quote.

---

## 3. Supplier Management

### Supplier Portal
Suppliers can log in to a secure portal to:
- Receive and respond to RFQs.
- Upload certificates (RoHS, REACH).
- Provide shipping notifications (ASN).

### Supplier Scorecards
Sensei OS automatically generates monthly scorecards based on:
- **Quality**: PPM and NCR counts.
- **Delivery**: On-Time Delivery (OTD) percentage.
- **Commercial**: Price competitiveness.

---

## 4. Purchasing Workflow (Standard)

1. **Requisition**: Review internal PRs from Production or Engineering.
2. **Purchase Order**: Generate and send POs.
3. **Tracking**: Monitor PO status and expedite late shipments.
4. **Receipt**: Verify goods receipts vs. PO terms.
