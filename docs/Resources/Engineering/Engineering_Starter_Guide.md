# Engineering Starter Guide

## Sensei OS - Engineering (EE, ME, Embedded) Complete Reference

---

## 1. Welcome & Role Overview

As an **Engineer** in Sensei OS, you provide the technical foundation for both new opportunities and ongoing production. Your primary interaction with the system occurs through the **Quoting Helper** (for technical reviews) and the **Obeya Room** (for NPI project execution).

### Key Responsibilities:
- Conduct **DFM/DFA reviews** via parallel engineering work packets.
- Define **Critical to Quality (CTQ)** parameters for new products.
- Manage **Bill of Materials (BOM)** and **Routings**.
- Execute **New Product Introduction (NPI)** milestones.
- Provide technical resolution for shop floor **Andon** alerts.

---

## 2. Quoting & RFQ Analysis (Stage-Gate)

When a new industrial opportunity is received, the system generates work packets for relevant disciplines.

### Disciplines & Tasks:
- **Electronics Engineer (EE)**: Analyze PCB complexity, fine pitch requirements, and need for X-ray inspection.
- **Embedded/Firmware Engineer**: Estimate programming time and identify fixture or IP security constraints.
- **Hardware/Mechanical Engineer (ME)**: Evaluate enclosure fit risks and labeling/laser marking methods.

### How to contribute:
1. Access the **Quoting Workbench** from the RFQ or Today Screen.
2. Open your assigned **Review Packet**.
3. Provide structured **Technical Inputs**.
4. Log **Internal Notes** for the Estimator.
5. **Sign Off** to unblock the cost rollup.

---

## 3. New Product Introduction (NPI)

Once a quote is won, it is converted into an NPI project.

### NPI Workflow:
1. **BOM Freeze**: Finalize the versioned BOM used during quoting.
2. **Process Route**: Define the sequence of operations (Station assignments).
3. **Inspection Plan**: Define the CTQs and sample sizes for quality gates.
4. **Traveler Generation**: Create the digital traveler for shop floor execution.

---

## 4. Shop Floor Support

### Andon Response
When a technical abnormality occurs on the floor (e.g., component fit issue, fixture failure), an Andon alert is sent to the relevant engineering discipline.
- View real-time shop floor status.
- Provide immediate disposition or remote coaching.
- Trigger **A3 Problem Solving** if the issue is recurring.
