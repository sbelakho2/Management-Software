# Finance Department AI Insights Access Guide

## Financial Intelligence at Your Fingertips

As a Finance team member, you have comprehensive access to all financial and cost-related AI insights. Your access is designed to support financial planning, cost control, and reporting activities.

---

## Your Insight Access Summary

| Category | Access Level | Details |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| Financial & Cost Analysis | ✅ Full | All financial metrics and forecasts |
| Production & Operations | ⚠️ Cost-Related | Cost aspects of production |
| Inventory & Supply Chain | ⚠️ Cost-Related | Inventory valuation, procurement costs |
| Quality & Compliance | ⚠️ Cost of Quality | Quality cost metrics |
| Workforce & HR | ❌ Limited | Labor costs only, not compensation details |
| Strategic & Executive | ⚠️ Partial | Financial aspects of strategic planning |

---

## Your Insight Categories

### 💰 Financial & Cost Analysis (Full Access)

| Insight | Description | Primary Use |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Cost Analysis | Detailed cost breakdowns | Variance analysis |
| Profitability Analysis | Product/customer margins | Portfolio decisions |
| Margin Trends | Historical margin analysis | Trend identification |
| Revenue Forecasts | AI revenue predictions | Budget planning |
| Cash Flow Insights | Cash position analysis | Treasury management |
| Budget Variance | Actual vs. budget | Monthly reviews |
| Activity-Based Costing | ABC cost allocation | Process improvement |
| Cost of Quality | Prevention/appraisal/failure costs | Quality investment |

**Best Practices:**
- Review Cash Flow Insights daily
- Analyze Profitability weekly by product line
- Generate Margin Trends reports monthly

### 📦 Inventory & Supply Chain (Cost Focus)

| Insight | What You See | Financial Use |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Inventory Valuation | Stock value by category | Balance sheet accuracy |
| Inventory Turnover | Turnover ratios | Working capital optimization |
| Procurement Costs | Supplier pricing trends | Budget forecasting |
| Cost Optimization | Savings opportunities | Cost reduction initiatives |

### 🏭 Production (Cost Aspects)

| Insight | What You See | Financial Use |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Production Costs | Per-unit costs, overhead | Cost accounting |
| Downtime Costs | Financial impact of downtime | Loss quantification |
| Capacity Cost Analysis | Fixed vs. variable costs | Break-even analysis |

### ✅ Quality (Cost of Quality)

| Insight | What You See | Financial Use |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Quality Costs | COPQ breakdown | Quality investment ROI |
| Scrap & Rework Costs | Waste costs | Loss prevention |
| Warranty Costs | Customer returns | Reserve planning |

### 👥 Workforce (Labor Costs Only)

| Insight | What You See | What's Hidden |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Labor Cost Analysis | Total labor costs | Individual salaries |
| Overtime Costs | OT spending | HR-specific data |
| Productivity Costs | Cost per output | Performance reviews |

## Rate Limits

Your access supports financial analysis workflows:
- **2,000 requests per minute**
- **20,000 requests per hour**
- **Burst: 100 in 10 seconds**

These limits support:
- Report generation
- Dashboard refreshes
- Month-end close activities
- Ad-hoc analysis

## What You Don't Have Access To

| Category | Why | Who Has It |
|----------|-----|------------|

| Strategic Recommendations | Executive level | CEO/Executives |
