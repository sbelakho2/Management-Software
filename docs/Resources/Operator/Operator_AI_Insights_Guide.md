# Operator AI Insights Access Guide

## Shop Floor AI Insights for Your Work Area

As an Operator, you have focused access to insights that help you do your job effectively. You can see production efficiency and equipment health for your assigned machines and work areas.

---

## Your Insight Access Summary

| Category | Access Level | Details |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| Production & Operations | ⚠️ Limited | Efficiency metrics for your area |
| Quality & Compliance | ⚠️ Limited | Quality metrics for your work |
| Maintenance & Equipment | ⚠️ Limited | Health of assigned equipment |
| All Other Categories | ❌ None | Contact supervisor for other data |

---

## Your Insight Categories

### 🏭 Production Efficiency

**What You See:**
- OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) for your machines
- Cycle times vs. targets
- Throughput for your work center
- Downtime for your equipment

**Why This Matters:**
- Track your performance against targets
- Identify when machines are running slow
- See impact of changeovers and breaks
- Understand your contribution to daily goals

**How to Use:**
1. Check at shift start for targets
2. Monitor during shift for issues
3. Review at shift end for summary

### ✅ Quality Metrics

**What You See:**
- Defect rates for your work
- First pass yield for your operations
- Quality alerts for your products

**Why This Matters:**
- Ensure quality targets are met
- Catch problems early
- Track your quality performance

**How to Use:**
1. Check quality alerts at shift start
2. Monitor defect trends during production
3. Report any quality issues immediately

### 🔧 Equipment Health

**What You See:**
- Status of your assigned machines
- Basic health indicators
- Maintenance alerts for your equipment

**Why This Matters:**
- Know when equipment needs attention
- Prevent unexpected breakdowns
- Schedule maintenance during breaks

**How to Use:**
1. Check equipment status at shift start
2. Note any warnings or alerts
3. Report issues to maintenance

## Rate Limits

Your access is optimized for shop floor use:
- **500 requests per minute**
- **5,000 requests per hour**
- **Burst: 50 in 10 seconds**

These limits support:
- Tablet dashboard refreshing every 10 seconds
- Checking status throughout shift
- Start/end of shift data review

## Common Questions

### "Why can't I see other machines?"
Your view is focused on your assigned equipment so you can concentrate on your work. Ask your supervisor for plant-wide data.

### "Why can't I see my performance history?"
Historical analysis is available through your supervisor. You see current shift data.

### "What if I see a red alert?"
1. Note the alert details
2. Inform your supervisor immediately
3. Follow standard procedures for that alert type

### "Can I customize my dashboard?"
Work with your supervisor to adjust the metrics shown on your workstation tablet.
